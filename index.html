## RULES COMPENDIUM

Source: official Cyberpunk TCG Alpha Gameplay Guide (WeirdCo). Confirmed to the Alpha ruleset — retail release may adjust values. If a card's text conflicts with the guide, the card wins.

WIN CONDITION

  • 7 Gigs to win: start your turn with 7+ Gig dice in your Gig area and you win instantly.
  • Overtime: after the last player's 7th turn, sudden death — a majority of Gigs wins instantly.
  • Deckout: if you must draw with an empty deck, your Rival wins.

TURN STRUCTURE

  1. Start Phase (in order): ready spent cards → draw 1 → gain a Gig (roll a die from your Fixer area, any except the d20, which is always last).
  2. Main Phase (any order): sell one card for an Eddie (once/turn) → play Units / Gear / Programs → Call a Legend (1 €$, once/turn) → attack with Units.

COMBAT

  • Attacking spends the Unit. Target a spent rival Unit (fight: compare power, loser is trashed, ties kill both) or the rival Gig area (steal a Gig).
  • Steal scaling: power 1+ = 1 Gig, 10+ = 2, 20+ = 3…
  • Reactions: Quick effects and Blocker Units can interrupt attacks. Ready Units can't be attacked.

ECONOMY — EDDIES (€$)

  • Once per turn, sell a card from hand (any with a sell tag) face-down into your Eddies area: worth 1 €$.
  • Legends (face-up or face-down) can each be spent as 1 €$.
  • Call a Legend: 1 €$ to flip one face-up at random, once per turn.

CARD TYPES

  • Legend — your deck's 3 centerpiece characters; random face-down order.
  • Unit — crew members; enter with Lag (can't attack the turn played).
  • Program — one-shot effect, then to trash.
  • Gear — attach to Units/Legends; goes with them when they move.

DECK BUILDING — RAM

  • Your 3 Legends provide colored RAM; each card needs RAM of matching colors.
  • Your Legends' total RAM must cover every card in the deck — the Legends define your color identity.
  • Colors: Red / Blue / Green / Yellow.

GLOSSARY (SELECTED)

Street Cred
Sum of your Gig dice values; unlocks powerful card effects.
Lag
All Units enter with it — can't attack or self-spend the turn they're played.
Go Solo
Pay a Legend's cost to play it as a ready Unit; if it leaves the field, remove it from the game.
Quick
May be activated / played as a reaction when a rival Unit attacks.
Blocker
Spend this Unit to redirect an attack to it.
Bottom-Deck
Put cards at the bottom of your deck in any order.
Trash
Put the top card of your deck into your trash (trashed N if a number is given).
Fixer Area
Where all your dice start; feed one into your Gig area each turn.

## FAQ — RELEASE & PRODUCT

Source: official Kickstarter FAQ at cyberpunktcg.com/faq.

When do Kickstarter rewards ship?

Two waves. Wave 1 (starter decks, Beta booster displays — from Netrunner Starter Kit tier and up) begins fulfillment Q3 2026 express. Wave 2 (accessories, Beta Box, Lucy cards, Common Cyberdecks tier) begins Q4 2026 standard.

What's in the Beta Box?

Free for Netrunner Starter Pack tier and above: 1 booster pack, zoned rubber playmat, all stretch-goal Legends, and more — grows as stretch goals unlock.

Kickstarter vs retail differences?

Kickstarter decks carry a Beta symbol; retail products won't. KS Beta products include exclusive art variants, and each starter deck has 5 mechanically unique cards exclusive to that product.

Free Lucy promo card?

Email newsletter signups before Jan 21, 2026 get a non-foil Nova Rare Lucy; campaign followers who backed get a foil Nova Rare Lucy with rewards.

Which languages?

Kickstarter cards are English-only; more languages (French confirmed in progress) are planned.

Try before retail?

Yes — official Print & Play demo decks: The Heist (Mercs) and Embracing Power (Arasaka), free PDFs on cyberpunktcg.com.
